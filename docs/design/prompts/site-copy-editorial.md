# Site copy editorial prompt

Goal and use case: Edit Crealize corporate-site copy so a prospective client or
collaborator immediately understands what the studio does and what to do next.

Target surface and dimensions: Responsive single-page site at `/`, `/ja/`, and
`/zh/`; copy must fit a 390px mobile viewport and desktop layouts.

Audience and moment: People evaluating Crealize's capabilities, work, process,
or open roles.

Approved brand truths: Crealize is an independent product studio in Tokyo. The
team researches, designs, builds, launches, and improves digital products.

Exact text, spelling, and language: Preserve `Crealize`, `Crealize LLC`,
`Creative × Realize`, product names, and `support@crealize.llc`. Provide natural
English, Japanese, and Traditional Chinese rather than literal translations.

Visual hierarchy and composition: Hero promise first; concrete vision; selected
work; a four-step method; then a direct contact or collaboration invitation.

Typography requirements: Do not change typefaces, weights, sizes, or tokens.

Color and contrast requirements: Do not change the existing token contract.

Image, lighting, material, or illustration direction: Not applicable; do not
create or replace imagery for copy-only work.

Required states or variants: Desktop and mobile; navigation closed/open;
language menu closed/open; English, Japanese, and Traditional Chinese.

Must preserve: Verified product facts, status, safety disclaimers, existing
routes, email behavior, and design tokens.

Must avoid: Abstract metaphors, swagger, strawman competitors, unsupported
claims, repeated em-dash slogans, “AI voice,” and promises not backed by current
evidence.

Accessibility and localization constraints: Controls need clear labels and
accurate expanded state. Text must not cause horizontal overflow at 390px.

Output format and transparent/background behavior: UTF-8 HTML/JavaScript copy;
no visual asset output.

Evaluation checklist: Reads naturally in each locale; says what Crealize does;
keeps facts aligned; fits mobile; contains no deprecated phrases; passes nav,
prerender, work, and design-token validators.

Source assets and rights/provenance: Existing repository copy and current live
site, edited by Codex on 2026-08-09. No new third-party asset or generated image.
