# Crealize brand and editorial contract

## Product promise

Crealize is an independent product studio in Tokyo. We research, design, build,
launch, and improve digital products. The site should make that work easy to
understand for potential clients and collaborators.

## Voice

- Clear, calm, capable, and human.
- Prefer concrete verbs and observable work: research, design, build, launch,
  measure, and improve.
- Keep sentences short enough to translate naturally into Japanese and
  Traditional Chinese.
- Use `Creative × Realize` and the established 0 → 1 idea sparingly; they are
  brand context, not a substitute for explaining the work.
- Case studies must preserve verified product facts, limitations, status, and
  safety language.

## Avoid

- Abstract metaphors that require interpretation, such as ideas being vapor,
  teams being constellations, or software being literature.
- Performative opposition such as “not résumés,” “allergic to theater,” or
  claims that unnamed competitors all behave badly.
- Generic AI-copy rhythms: repeated em dashes, three-part slogans, rhetorical
  “the unusual part is,” and unsupported superlatives.
- Claims about market leadership, customer outcomes, company culture, freedom,
  or availability unless current evidence supports them.
- Literal translation when a native Japanese or Traditional Chinese sentence
  would be clearer.

## Localization

English, Japanese, and Traditional Chinese must communicate the same facts and
call to action, but each version should read as native copy. Product names,
company name, email, verified platform status, and safety disclaimers remain
unchanged unless their owning evidence changes.

## Interface copy

- Controls name the action or destination.
- Navigation is available but not persistently expanded on narrow screens.
- Language selection must expose all three locales, announce expanded state,
  close on a second activation or Escape, and remain operable at 390px.
- Form success and error text must describe what actually happened. The current
  form opens the visitor's mail application; it does not submit to a backend.

## Visual and asset boundaries

This editorial update does not change the logo, colors, fonts, imagery, or
motion system. Those remain owned by
`docs/design-system/tokens/crealize.tokens.json`, `site/css/tokens.css`, and the
approved assets under `site/assets/`.
