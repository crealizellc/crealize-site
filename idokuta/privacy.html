iDokuta Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

iDokuta ("we", "our", or "the Service") is operated by Crealize LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use the iDokuta mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide our service:

  • Account information: Name, email address, preferred language, and country of residence.
  • Health information: Symptoms and medical history you share during consultations. This data is used solely to connect you with a licensed physician.
  • Communication data: Messages exchanged between you and physicians through our in-app translation system.
  • Payment information: Subscription and payment records (processed by third-party payment providers; we do not store full card numbers).
  • Device information: Device type, operating system, and app version for technical support purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To connect you with licensed Japanese physicians
  • To provide real-time text translation between you and your doctor
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions
  • To deliver electronic prescriptions and facilitate medication delivery
  • To improve the accuracy and quality of our translation service
  • To comply with Japanese medical and privacy laws

3. Information Sharing

We share your information only with:

  • Licensed physicians providing your consultation
  • Pharmacies and delivery services to fulfill prescriptions (with your consent)
  • Payment processors for billing
  • Government authorities when required by law

We do not sell your personal or health information to third parties.

4. Health Data

Health information you share is treated with the highest level of confidentiality. Consultation records are retained for the legally required period under Japanese law (typically 5 years from the last consultation) and then securely deleted.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption (TLS in transit, AES-256 at rest) to protect your data. All data is stored in secure data centers compliant with applicable privacy standards.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Object to certain processing activities

To exercise these rights, contact us at: privacy@crealize.llc

7. Account Deletion & Data Removal

iDokuta — Account and Data Deletion Request

You may request the deletion of your iDokuta account and all associated personal data at any time. To do so:

  1. Open the iDokuta app and go to Settings → My Account.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm your identity and submit the deletion request.
  4. You will receive a confirmation email at your registered address within 7 days.

Alternatively, email us at privacy@crealize.llc with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and include your registered email address. We will process your request within 30 days.

What is deleted: Your account profile, contact details, language preferences, and in-app messages. Health consultation records are retained for the legally required period under Japanese law (typically 5 years from the last consultation) and then securely deleted. Payment transaction records required for legal compliance are retained as required by applicable law.

Partial data deletion: You may also request deletion of specific data types (e.g., messages or health records older than a specified date) without deleting your full account. Email privacy@crealize.llc with your specific request.

8. Children's Privacy

iDokuta is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes through the app or by email. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
